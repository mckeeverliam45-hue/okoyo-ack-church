OUR STORY

History of ACK Okoyo Church

The History of Seme and Our Community

The story of Seme is a rich history of ancestry, migration, settlement, family, friendship, marriage and community. Over generations, different families and communities travelled, settled and became connected to one another.

Kagumba and Kadipir

Traditional history associates two major ancestral groupings with Seme: Kagumba and Kadipir. The traditional genealogy traces these groupings to the wider family of Odongo, Gumba and Dipir.

Migration and Settlement

The movement of families across different parts of the region played an important role in shaping Seme. Different families arrived through different routes and became part of the wider community through settlement, family relationships, friendship and marriage.

A Community of Many Histories

The history of Seme is therefore a story of many journeys. Different families and communities contributed to the rich cultural heritage of the area.

Our Heritage

ACK Okoyo Church is part of this wider community heritage. As a church family, we recognise the importance of remembering our history, respecting our communities and building a future founded on faith, love, unity and service.

Anglican identity

Part of the Anglican Church of Kenya and the wider Diocese of Maseno South.

Community & fellowship

A place for worship, Christian formation, prayer and service in Oluti Village.

Building for the future
